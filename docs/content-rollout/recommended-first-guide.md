## Why it is preferred over alternatives
`/needs/first-days-checklist` remains the highest justified first guide.
- **User Impact:** EVERY newly arrived individual requires the 72-hour checklist. It bridges the gap between landing and establishing legal presence.
- **Legal or Factual Risk:** Medium. Incorrect advice on declaring residence to IGI within 3 days or ANAF registration can lead to immediate fines.
- **Content Gap:** The current implementation is superficial and lacks strict, authoritative timelines.
- **Dependency Value:** Foundational. You cannot get housing without status; you cannot get banking without housing/status. This must come first.
- **Implementation Effort:** Moderate. Easily maps to the `OperationalGuideLayout` established in the pilot.
