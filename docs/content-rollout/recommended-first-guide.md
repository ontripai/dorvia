# Recommended First Guide After Pilot

**Selected Route:** `/needs/first-days-checklist`

## Reassessment and Comparison
When comparing `/needs/first-days-checklist`, `/needs/health`, `/needs/housing`, `/needs/banking`, and `/needs/telecom`:

- **User Impact:** EVERY newly arrived individual requires the 72-hour checklist. It bridges the gap between landing and establishing legal presence.
- **Legal or Factual Risk:** Medium/High. Incorrect advice on declaring residence to IGI within 3 days or ANAF registration can lead to immediate fines.
- **Current Content Gap:** The current implementation is superficial and lacks strict, step-by-step authoritative timelines.
- **Dependency Value:** Foundational. You cannot get housing without status; you cannot get banking without housing/status. This must come first.
- **Implementation Effort:** Moderate. Easily maps to the `OperationalGuideLayout` established in the pilot.
- **Ability to Validate Reusable System:** Perfect fit for `StepByStepProcess` and `ImportantWarnings`.

## Official Authorities Required
- IGI (General Inspectorate for Immigration)
- ANAF (Tax Authority)
- Local City Halls (Primăria)

## Why it is preferred over alternatives
While `/needs/housing` or `/needs/banking` are critical, they depend structurally on the steps outlined in the first days (e.g., obtaining a CNP or registration certificate). Standardizing the first days checklist creates the necessary authoritative flow.
