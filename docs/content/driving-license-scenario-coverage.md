# Driving License Scenario Coverage

## 1. temporary-foreign-licence-use
- **Applies to**: Tourists, short-term visa holders.
- **Coverage**: Addressed IDP requirements and residency limitations.

## 2. foreign-licence-exchange
- **Applies to**: Foreigners from Annex 1 states (OMAI 163/2011).
- **Coverage**: Requirements, fees, medical, without exam.

## 3. iranian-issued-licence
- **Applies to**: Iranian citizens holding valid Iranian license.
- **Coverage**: Embassy verification, translation, medical, 89 RON exchange fee, timeline varies.

## 4. obtain-romanian-licence-from-scratch
- **Applies to**: Foreigners with non-exchangeable or expired licenses.
- **Coverage**: Driving school, psychological test, theory, and practical exam.

## 5. renew-romanian-licence
- **Applies to**: Holders of a Romanian Driving License approaching expiry.
- **Coverage**: Medical requirements, DGPCI procedures, 89 RON fee.

## 6. international-driving-permit
- **Applies to**: Tourists needing an IDP.
- **Coverage**: Basic eligibility.

## 7. penalties-suspension-and-restrictions
- **Applies to**: Any driver.
- **Coverage**: Codul Rutier basics.

**Validation Status**: 7/7 Scenarios fully registered in EN and FA data models.
