# Source & Claim Policy

## Principle of Verifiability
Every material factual claim regarding:
- State Fees
- Administrative Deadlines
- Legal Requirements
- Mandatory Documents

Must be tied to a primary official source or explicitly qualified if derived from secondary practice.

## Forbidden Wording
- تضمینی (Guaranteed)
- صددرصد (100% certainty)
- بدون مشکل (Without issues)
