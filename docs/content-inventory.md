## 2. Forbidden Promotional Claims Audit

The following promotional exaggerations are **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** and have been audited out of prose:
- `تضمینی` (Guaranteed)
- `صددرصد` (100%)
- `فوری` (Immediate)
- `پذیرش قطعی` (Guaranteed Admission)
- `اقامت تجاری` (Commercial Residence - misleading)
- `اقامت از طریق ثبت شرکت` (Residence via registration alone)
- `مالیات ۱٪` (1% Tax - qualified by turnover/staff conditions)
- `WHO approved` (WHO does not issue university approvals)
- `معتبرترین` / `بهترین` (Superlatives without cited methodology)

## 3. Official Source Allowlist

All content claims are tied to primary official sources:
1. **Financial & Currency**: National Bank of Romania (BNR `bnr.ro`), Ministry of Finance (`mfinante.ro`).
2. **Immigration & Residency**: General Inspectorate for Immigration (IGI `igi.mai.gov.ro`), Ministry of Foreign Affairs (`mae.ro`).
3. **Education & Admissions**: Ministry of Education (`edu.ro`), official university portals.
4. **Business & Companies**: National Trade Register Office (ONRC `onrc.ro`), ANAF (`anaf.ro`).
5. **Driving & Traffic**: DGPCI (`dgpci.mai.gov.ro`), MAI (`mai.gov.ro`).
6. **Translation & Legal**: Ministry of Justice (`just.ro`), National Union of Public Notaries (`uniuneanotarilor.ro`).
7. **Consular & Iranian Services**: Embassy of Iran in Bucharest (`bucharest.mfa.ir`), Mikhak (`mikhak.mfa.gov.ir`).
8. **Real Estate**: ANCPI (`ancpi.ro`), eTerra.
