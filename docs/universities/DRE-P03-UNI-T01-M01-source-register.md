# University Source Register

**Task ID**: DRE-P03-UNI-T01-M01

The following sources were researched and bound to the new University data model.

1. **Carol Davila University of Medicine and Pharmacy (UMFCD)**
   - Used: https://umfcd.ro/
   - Tuition Data: Historical 2024-25 figures (EUR 10,000 for Medicine, EUR 8,500 for Pharmacy). Lacks current EUR publications for 2026-27.

2. **Victor Babeș University of Medicine and Pharmacy (UMFT)**
   - Used: https://www.umft.ro/
   - Tuition Data: Withheld from public international portal. Set to `CONTACT_UNIVERSITY`.

3. **Grigore T. Popa University of Medicine and Pharmacy (UMF Iasi)**
   - Used: https://www.umfiasi.ro/
   - Tuition Data: Withheld from public international portal. Set to `CONTACT_UNIVERSITY`.

4. **Iuliu Hațieganu University of Medicine and Pharmacy (UMF Cluj)**
   - Used: https://umfcluj.ro/
   - Tuition Data: Withheld from public international portal. Set to `CONTACT_UNIVERSITY`.

5. **University of Bucharest (UniBuc)**
   - Used: https://international.unibuc.ro
   - Tuition Data: Range EUR 2,500–3,150/year.

6. **Politehnica Bucharest (UPB)**
   - Used: https://international.upb.ro/assets/docs/2026/regulations/POLITEHNICA_Bucharest_tuition_fees.pdf
   - Tuition Data: Range EUR 2,200–7,500/year.

7. **Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE)**
   - Used: https://international.ase.ro
   - Tuition Data: Fixed EUR 3,500 for BSc, EUR 4,500 for MSc. Explicit EUR 350/450 application fees.

8. **Romanian-American University (RAU)**
   - Used: https://rau.ro/tuition-fees-and-finances
   - Tuition Data: EUR 50 initial registration fee, tuition depends on direct contact. Noted visa refusal refund policy.

9. **Titu Maiorescu University (TMU)**
   - Used: https://www.utm.ro/
   - Tuition Data: Estimated at around EUR 11,000/year. Unofficial estimate. Not MOH approved.
